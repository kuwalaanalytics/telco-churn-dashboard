# Brief: churn dashboard explainer

- **Who it is for:** owners and managers of telecom, internet and other subscription businesses, and people hiring a data analyst.
- **The one thing it says:** your customer records can show who is about to leave, and this dashboard puts that on one screen.
- **Length:** 40.5 seconds (v2). v1 was 28.6 seconds but typed lines while pictures were still moving.
- **Format:** portrait, 1080 by 1920, 60 frames a second.
- **Where it goes:** LinkedIn first, then Instagram and Facebook.
- **Opens on:** "Which customers are about to leave you?"
- **The ask at the end:** "Do you know who is about to leave?" This is also the pinned comment.
- **End card:** top to bottom, the full Kuwala Analytics logo, the LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram icons, "Contact us for your data needs", and "Demo on public IBM sample data". The dashboard address goes in the post caption and pinned comment instead.
- **Sound:** music and effects only in the MP4. The voice is recorded afterwards in CapCut.
- **Honesty:** every figure comes from the public IBM Telco sample dataset. The label "Demo on public IBM sample data" shows with the dashboard scene and again on the end card.

## What it shows, in order

1. The question.
2. The real dashboard.
3. One in four customers left (churn rate 26.5%).
4. The revenue that went with them ($139,131 a month).
5. Month to month customers leave most (42.7%).
6. New customers leave fastest (52.9% in the first 6 months).
7. The ranked list of customers to call.
8. The top 20% by risk holds about half of the leavers (51%).
9. The question again, as the ask.
10. Logo, the contact line, the name and the social icons.

The lines and their timings are in `SCRIPT.md`.
