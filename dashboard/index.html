Classes were balanced during training so the model does not ignore the smaller group of leavers. A simpler logistic regression scored almost the same, so the result does not depend on one technique.

Recommended Retention Actions

Based on the full customer base (not affected by filters)

1. Win the first six months

52.9% of customers in their first 6 months leave, against 9.5% after 4 years. Add a welcome call, a 30 day check-in and a 90 day review for every new customer.

2. Move month-to-month customers onto contracts

Month-to-month churn is 42.7%, versus 11.3% on one year and 2.8% on two year contracts. Offer a discount or free add-on for committing to 12 months.

3. Bundle online security and tech support

Internet customers with online security churn at 14.6% versus 41.8% without; tech support shows 15.2% versus 41.6%. Trial these free for 3 months with customers who have no add-ons.

4. Shift electronic check payers to automatic payment

Electronic check payers churn at 45.3%, about three times the 15.2% to 16.7% seen on automatic payments. Give a small bill credit for switching.

5. Review the fibre optic experience and price

Fibre customers churn at 41.9% and account for $114,300 of the $139,131 lost each month. Survey leavers and check price and service quality against competitors.

6. Call the highest-risk customers every week

The top 20% of customers by risk score contained 51% of all leavers in a back-test. If a retention offer kept 30% of them, that is about $269,664 a year (the 30% save rate is an assumption, not a measured result).
